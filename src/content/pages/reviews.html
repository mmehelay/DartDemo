Our Services

Professional Construction Services

D.A.R.T SERVICES offers a comprehensive suite of services designed to cater to your specific needs. With a commitment to excellence, safety, and sustainability, we deliver top-notch results that exceed expectations. Explore our range of services and discover how we can make your project a resounding success:

  • Demolition Services

    Whether it's the removal of structures, interiors, or specialized demolition needs, we handle it all with expertise and care.

  • Cleanup and Restoration

    From post-renovation cleaning to sewer backup decontamination, we leave spaces spotless and safe.

  • Asbestos Abatement

    Our asbestos abatement and removal services ensure that hazardous asbestos materials are handled with utmost care.

  • General Contracting

    We offer a wide range of general contracting services that are designed to meet the diverse needs of our clients.

  • Painting

    Our painting services enhance the aesthetics and longevity of your structures with high-quality finishes.

  • Waterproofing

    Our waterproofing solutions protect your structures from water damage, ensuring durability and safety.

  • House Cleaning

    From routine home cleaning to deep cleaning after construction or renovation, we provide thorough house cleaning services.

  • Hardwood Floor Finishing/Staining

    We specialize in the finishing and staining of hardwood floors, delivering a durable and polished look for your interiors.

  • Deck Finishing/Staining

    Our deck finishing and staining services protect your outdoor spaces while enhancing their appearance and durability.
